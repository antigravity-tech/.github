# Antigravity Tech Team @ Insta360

We are the algorithm research team behind Antigravity, incubated by Insta360. Our GitHub organization hosts the official implementations of our research in **panoramic vision, depth estimation, stereo matching, 3D reconstruction and generation**.

We open-source our work to support the research community and push the boundaries of what's possible with panoramic and immersive visual computing.

## Research Areas

- Panoramic depth estimation
- Stereo matching & depth foundation models
- Panoramic image generation
- 3D view synthesis

## Open Source Projects

- [DA360](https://github.com/antigravity-tech/DA360) — Depth Anything in 360°: Towards Scale Invariance in the Wild
- [DEFOM-Stereo](https://github.com/antigravity-tech/DEFOM-Stereo) — Depth foundation model based stereo matching (CVPR 2025)
- [RomniStereo](https://github.com/antigravity-tech/RomniStereo) — Recurrent Omnidirectional Stereo Matching (IEEE RA-L)
